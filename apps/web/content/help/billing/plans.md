# What's free and what isn't

**Free, permanently:** the whole [drill library](/drills), every drill page, and
the board itself. No account needed to use any of it.

**An account adds** saved drills, practice plans, and a public coach page.

**Paying** lifts the limits — unlimited saved drills and plans, more teams, and
importing a drill from a photo of a whiteboard. The current numbers are on the
[pricing page](/pricing); it's the same table the app enforces, so it can't
disagree with what you actually get.

## Cancelling

Cancel from **Account → Billing** at any time. You keep everything you've made;
the limits go back to the free plan's, and nothing is deleted.
