# Draw your first drill

The board has one idea behind it: **you draw where people go, and it works out
when they get there.** Timing comes from the length of each route, not from you
setting numbers.

## The short version

1. **Add a player.** Tap the ice with the player tool. They land where you tap.
2. **Give them a route.** Drag from the player. Each drag adds a leg; the route
   is what the animation follows.
3. **Add a puck.** Drop it on a player and it becomes theirs — the puck follows
   whoever is carrying it.
4. **Press play.** Everything moves at once, at speeds derived from the real
   distances on a 200 by 85 foot sheet.

## Then the useful part

- **Passes and shots** are properties of the puck, not separate objects. Tap the
  puck to say who it goes to and at which point in the route.
- **Notes** are markdown, and they print. A drill with good coaching points is
  worth ten drills without them.
- **Share** produces a link that carries the whole drill in the URL. No account
  needed on either end.

When it looks right, use **Share preview link** and send it to yourself — that's
the same link format every drill in [the library](/drills) uses.
